File no. 001 — Clément Bonnand

If I had to define myself in one word, I'd say creative. I use it to make every project unique — and different.

I'm 23. I grew up on Saint-Martin in the Caribbean for eleven years, learned to surf before I knew what a brand book was, and ended up in the marketing team of DC Shoes Europe on the Basque coast — connecting collections with skaters, artists and the people who make culture move.

Driven by many passions, I like to see projects through to the very end, stacking every chance on my side to turn ambitions into results. I'm wired to sport, fashion and culture, with a real appetite for ideas and for activating new talent.

My story

From an island in the Caribbean to the Atlantic swell

Chapter 01 — 18°04′N 63°03′W

Saint
Martin

0years on the island

I grew up in Saint-Martin, French West Indies — eleven years of turquoise water, trade winds and music coming out of every car window.

An island teaches you things a classroom can't: mixing cultures and languages every day, talking to anyone, and knowing that the best ideas usually show up barefoot. My eye for colour, my love for reggae and rap, my obsession with the ocean — it all starts there.

Chapter 02 — Landes & Basque coast

Salt
water

surf, school, first brand

Back in mainland France, I followed the swell to the Atlantic coast.

Surf shaped the way I work: read the conditions, commit, fall, paddle back out. In 2024 it even became my first business — Surfattitude, a surf-poncho brand I designed, produced and sold myself on the Landes markets. Today I'm on the Basque coast, inside DC Shoes, where board culture is the job.
